CHAPTER I

INTRODUCTION
This chapter will introduce the research problem, provide the project context, statement of objectives, purpose and description, outline the scope and limitations. It will also present the conceptual and theoretical framework used in the development of the system.
Project Context
Fish ports will remain critical economic centers that link fishermen, traders, retailers and consumers in the marine supply chain. Fishport will also have a vital role to play in feeding the nation and helping the Filipino people, as well as being a cornerstone for the fishermen along the coasts. Nevertheless, the majority of public fish ports will still make use of the manual monitoring systems, the traditional handwritten documentation and the pieced together reporting systems that can cause inefficiencies and a lack of data visibility.
Among these is the Lucena Fish Port Complex that will be developed into a regional fish trading center in Southern Luzon. The port will receive daily fish landings from its fleet as well as other fishing activities, and it will serve as a platform for fish products to be transported to retailers and local markets. Although essential for their operation, the identification of fish arrivals, the retailer information, sourcing, supply and reporting will be largely manual or semi-paperless.

Manual logbooks and spreadsheet monitoring can negatively impact operations through late reporting,, incomplete data entries, challenge in tracing the origin of the fish supply, or generally making market activities invisible in up-to-date. In addition, administrators may have difficulty in predicting fish availability, the days session will be busiest and demand patterns in the absence of foresight. 
It has been pointed out that equipment information, health status and record are highly integrated in supply chain and market surveillance system based on digital technology, and this combination enhances operational transparency and efficiency, and coordination among players (Ivanov et al., 2019). The adoption of web-based monitoring systems has led to a decrease in manual errors and an increase in visibility of up-to-date data, as well as the facilitation of expedited administrative decisions in the logistics and distribution industries (Kache & Seuring, 2017).
Especially, predictive analytics has been shown to not only improve demand forecasting accuracy but also reveal seasonal patterns in supply chains, enable more efficient resource utilization and storage allocation in the supply chain (Choi et al., 2018). Also, studies have demonstrated how data-driven forecasting models can assist firms in reducing waste, avoiding overstocking/understocking, and improving strategic planning in industries dealing with perishable goods such as agro-based products and fisheries (Mukhopadhyay et al., 2020).


The aforementioned studies back the advancement of FishLodger as the digital monitoring and predictive analytics solution enhancing the operational capabilities, transparency and decisions of those within the LFP.
With this, the researchers will develop FishLodger, a progressive web app up-to-date fish market monitoring system catered for the Lucena Fish Port complex. The system will combine retailer information management, supply source identification, automated reporting, and predictive analytics on a single digital platform. In this way, fish port managers can keep a pulse on routine activity, prepare accurate reports, and predict future supply patterns for improved planning and administration.

Scope and Limitations
This research is the design and development of FishLodger, a progressive web app fish monitoring system developed for Lucena Fish Port Complex. The system facilitates fish port boards to track fish market activities and fish port operation information.
The proposed system incorporates retailer data management, fish supply identification, retail supply source tracing, fish report automation and predictive analytics. These functionalities are designed to strengthen monitoring procedures and provide insight information on the trends of fish supplies and retailers in the fish port.
FishLodger is a web-based up-to-date fish monitoring software developed for Lucena Fish Port Complex. portrays the functional core of the system in terms of operational data such as fish supplies, retailer information and market monitoring.
Yet there are certain constraints to the system. The system is tailor-made for the Lucena Fish Port Complex and is not applicable to any other fish market in the country which has a different mode of operations. Since it is a web-based system, its effectiveness depends on the accuracy of data encoded by fish port administrators and the consistent participation of fish vendors in the system.  Furthermore, the predicted analytics analysis, which is based on patterns of historical data, does not take into consideration 

the impact of unexpected events such as extreme weather, sudden interruptions of supply or other external factors that might have an influence on the fish market activities. 
Purpose and Description
The purpose of this study will be to design and develop FishLodger, a progressive web-based monitoring system that will enhance the operational efficiency of the Lucena Fish Port Complex. The system will centralize fish market data into a single digital platform that will promote transparency, traceability, and data-driven decision-making.
FishLodger will record fish arrivals and quantities, manage retailer registration and profiles, track supply sources from fishing vessels, and generate daily, weekly, and monthly reports. Through predictive analytics, the system will forecast fish supply and demand patterns to assist administrators and stakeholders in planning operations more effectively.
The system will benefit fish port administrators and officers by providing an up-to-date dashboard of fish volume, supplier activity, and retailer transactions, enabling faster monitoring, accurate reporting, and improved operational planning. It will benefit retailers and vendors by providing structured registration, proper documentation, and organized transaction records that will simplify compliance and daily transactions. Fishermen and suppliers will also benefit from the system through proper identification and traceability of fish supplies delivered to the port, which will improve coordination and record accuracy. Decision-makers and managers will gain predictive insights into seasonality, high-demand species, and supply variations that will support better resource allocation, storage preparation, and workforce planning. Future researchers and system developers will also benefit as the study will serve as a reference model for implementing digital monitoring and predictive analytics systems in similar market environments.
Automated reports will eliminate manual data compilation, reducing errors and saving time for personnel. During peak fishing seasons, FishLodger will predict increased supply volumes and will help managers prepare storage space and workforce allocation. During low-supply periods, the system will assist in adjusting operational strategies.
FishLodger will aim to modernize fish port operations by transforming labor-intensive processes into a structured, intelligent, and data-driven system that will promote efficiency, accountability, and sustainability for all stakeholders involved in the fish market ecosystem.
The study will benefit the following:
Fish Port Administrators and Personnel – The system will help administrators monitor fish arrivals, retailer activities, and supply movements through an up-to-date dashboard and automated reports. It will improve operational efficiency, reduce manual recording tasks, and support faster and more accurate decision-making.
Retailers and Vendors – FishLodger will provide retailers and vendors with a structured registration and monitoring system that will help organize transaction records, retailer information, and market activities. This will simplify documentation processes and improve transaction management within the fish port.
Fishermen and Fish Suppliers – The system will assist fishermen and suppliers through proper identification and traceability of fish supplies delivered to the fish port. This will improve coordination, supply documentation, and monitoring of fish deliveries.
Fish Port Management and Decision-Makers – The predictive analytics feature of the system will provide insights into fish supply trends, seasonal demand patterns, and market activities. These insights will support better planning, resource allocation, workforce preparation, and storage management.
Consumers and Local Markets – By improving the monitoring and management of fish supplies, the system will contribute to a more organized and efficient distribution of fish products, which may support consistent market availability and supply transparency.
Future Researchers and System Developers – This study will serve as a reference for future studies related to fish market monitoring systems, predictive analytics, and web-based management systems. Future researchers and developers may use the findings and system design as a basis for improving or developing similar technologies in related industries.

By combining these notions, the FishLodger will focus on delivering a digital product that increases the monitoring process and decisions making within the fish port environment.

Definition of Terms
For this study, certain terms will be defined to convey specific meanings as used in the research. These definitions aim to reduce ambiguity and help readers better understand the concepts and system features presented.
Technical Terms
Automated Report Generation – A system feature that will generate summarized reports of fish market activities using recorded data. In this study, it will produce daily, 

weekly, and monthly reports on fish supply transactions, retailer activities, and market operations at the Lucena Fish Port Complex.
Fish Market Monitoring – The process of observing, recording, and tracking fish trading activities, supply movements, and retailer transactions within the fish port. In this study, FishLodger will facilitate this through a centralized digital system.
Predictive Analytics – A branch of data analytics that will use historical data and statistical techniques to forecast future trends or outcomes. In this study, it will be used to predict fish supply and demand patterns.
Retailer Information Management – The process of recording, storing, and maintaining information about fish retailers operating within the fish port, including their profiles, registration details, and transaction records.
Supply Source Identification – The process of identifying and recording the origin of fish supplies delivered to the fish port. The system will track fish supplies from fishing vessels and other sources for proper documentation and traceability.
Up-to-Date Monitoring – The capability of the system to record and display fish arrivals, retailer transactions, and supply movements as they occur.
Dashboard and Data Visualization – A system interface that will present fish market data in graphical and visual formats such as charts and summaries for easier understanding and monitoring.
Operational Terms
Fish Arrival Data – Information recorded in the system regarding the species, volume, and arrival time of fish delivered to the port.
Historical Data – Previously recorded fish supply and market data that will be used by the system for analysis and prediction.
Retailer Transaction Records – Data related to the buying activities of registered retailers within the fish port.
User – Refers to fish port administrators, personnel, and authorized individuals who will encode, manage, and monitor data using the system.
Web-Based System – A software application that will operate through a web browser and require internet connectivity to access its features.







CHAPTER II
REVIEW OF RELATED LITERATURE AND SYSTEMS 
This chapter will discuss the related concepts and findings from various literature and studies gathered from books, journals, and online resources to support the necessity of investigating and determining the impact of developing of a progressive web application fish market monitoring system for Lucena Fish Port Complex.
Fish Market Monitoring Systems in the Modern Day
Modern fish markets are gradually transitioning from traditional manual processes to digital monitoring systems. According to Cruz (2022), fish market monitoring systems are digital platforms designed to track fish transactions, inventory, and pricing, improving transparency and operational efficiency. In his study on “Digital Transformation in Coastal Market Systems,” it was revealed that manual recording methods in fish ports often lead to data inconsistencies, delays, and lack of transparency.
Similarly, Santos et al. (2023) found that the implementation of digital monitoring systems in fish ports increased operational efficiency by reducing reporting time and improving data accuracy. Their study highlighted that over seventy percent (70%) of fish market stakeholders preferred digital systems due to faster transaction recording and up-to-date monitoring capabilities.

These findings support the goal of the present study, which aims to develop a centralized monitoring system for the Lucena Fish Port Complex. The system is expected to improve transaction identification, enhance transparency, and provide up-to-date data access for stakeholders.
Progressive Web Applications in Information Systems
Progressive Web Applications (PWA) have emerged as a modern solution for delivering reliable, efficient, and accessible web-based systems. According to Sharma (2021), PWAs combine the functionality of both web and mobile applications, enabling users to access systems offline, receive up-to-date updates, and experience improved performance across various devices. This hybrid capability makes PWAs highly suitable for environments where consistent internet connectivity may not always be available.
Garcia and Lee (2022), in their study “Adoption of Progressive Web Applications in Enterprise Systems,” revealed that PWAs significantly reduce development and maintenance costs while maintaining high performance and accessibility. Their findings showed that organizations adopting PWAs experienced improved user engagement, faster loading times, and enhanced system reliability. Additionally, PWAs eliminate the need for separate mobile applications, allowing businesses to streamline their digital infrastructure while still providing a mobile-friendly experience.


Furthermore, a report by Tech Insights (2023) indicated that more than sixty percent (60%) of businesses have shifted toward PWA-based systems due to their scalability, cross-platform compatibility, and ease of deployment. The report also emphasized that PWAs contribute to increased operational efficiency by simplifying updates and reducing dependency on platform-specific development.
These studies collectively justify the use of a Progressive Web Application in the FishLodger system, as it ensures accessibility, flexibility, and efficiency for fish vendors, administrators, and stakeholders. By leveraging PWA technology, the system can function effectively even in areas with limited internet connectivity, while still providing a responsive and user-friendly experience.
Fish Supply Chain Identification and Traceability Systems
Traceability systems play a critical role in ensuring food safety, quality assurance, and transparency in the fisheries sector. According to Reyes (2024), fish supply chain identification systems enable stakeholders to monitor the movement of fish products from the point of catch to the final market destination, ensuring accountability and reducing the risk of fraud and mislabeling. These systems are essential in maintaining accurate records and promoting trust among consumers, suppliers, and regulatory agencies.
In a related study, Villanueva et al. (2022) developed a traceability system utilizing QR codes and mobile technology, allowing users to access detailed information 

about fish origin, handling processes, and distribution channels. The study revealed that such systems significantly improved consumer trust, strengthened regulatory compliance, and enhanced transparency within the supply chain. It also highlighted that digital traceability minimizes manual errors and simplifies data verification processes.
Additionally, a global review by Ahmed (2023) emphasized that traceability technologies, including Internet of Things (IoT) devices and digital databases, greatly enhance supply chain visibility and operational efficiency. The study pointed out that integrating these technologies allows up-to-date data collection and monitoring, which supports better decision-making and more efficient resource management within the fisheries sector.
These findings support the inclusion of a supply source identification feature in the FishLodger system, which aims to provide accurate, reliable, and transparent information about fish products within the Lucena Fish Port Complex. By integrating traceability functionalities, the system can improve accountability, strengthen stakeholder confidence, and ensure better management of fish supply chains.
Automated Report Generation Systems
Automated report generation systems are essential components of modern information systems because they improve efficiency, accuracy, and accessibility of operational data. According to Martinez (2022), automated reporting systems are designed to collect, process, and generate reports without requiring extensive manual intervention. These systems reduce human errors, minimize delays in report preparation, and ensure that stakeholders receive organized and accurate information for decision-making purposes.
In a related study, Torres and Ramirez (2023) emphasized that automated reporting significantly improves operational workflows by allowing organizations to generate daily, weekly, and monthly summaries in real time. Their findings revealed that businesses using automated reporting systems experienced faster report preparation, improved data consistency, and better monitoring of organizational performance. The study also highlighted that automated systems reduce paperwork and simplify record management processes.
Furthermore, Johnson (2024) stated that integrating automated report generation with centralized databases enhances data accessibility and transparency. By automatically consolidating transaction records and operational information, organizations can quickly analyze trends and monitor performance indicators. The study further explained that automated reporting systems support better strategic planning by providing timely and reliable reports for management and stakeholders.
These studies support the inclusion of automated report generation in the FishLodger system. Through automated reporting, the system can efficiently generate summaries of fish transactions, retailer activities, and supply monitoring records within the Lucena Fish Port Complex. This functionality can help administrators reduce manual workload, improve record accuracy, and enhance operational monitoring.


Philippine Fisheries Digitalization and BFAR Initiatives
The fisheries sector in the Philippines continues to face challenges related to manual monitoring systems, inconsistent data recording, and limited technological integration in municipal fish ports. According to the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) (2023), digital transformation initiatives are necessary to improve fisheries management, strengthen data collection, and enhance transparency in fishery operations. BFAR emphasized that accurate and timely fisheries data are essential in supporting sustainable fishery practices and effective resource management.
In a report by the Philippine Fisheries Development Authority (PFDA) (2022), many fish ports in the country still rely on manual processes for recording fish transactions, monitoring supply distribution, and generating operational reports. The report highlighted that manual systems often result in delayed reporting, data inconsistencies, and difficulty in tracking fish supply movement. It further recommended the adoption of digital monitoring systems to improve operational efficiency and data reliability in fish port facilities.
Similarly, Dela Cruz et al. (2024) examined the implementation of digital technologies in selected municipal fish ports in the Philippines. Their study found that the use of web-based monitoring systems improved transaction recording, increased transparency, and enhanced communication among stakeholders. However, the researchers noted that several local fish ports still lack integrated systems capable of combining monitoring, traceability, reporting, and analytics functionalities.
These studies strengthen the relevance of the proposed FishLodger system for the Lucena Fish Port Complex. By incorporating digital monitoring, automated reporting, and predictive analytics, the system aligns with the ongoing digitalization efforts promoted by BFAR and other fisheries-related agencies in the Philippines. The proposed system aims to address the operational gaps in local fish port management by providing a centralized, efficient, and accessible platform for stakeholders.
The Use of Predictive Analytics for Market and Business Optimization
Predictive analytics is widely used to forecast trends and improve decision-making across various industries. According to Kumar (2023), predictive analytics utilizes historical data, statistical techniques, and machine learning algorithms to anticipate future outcomes and optimize business processes. By analyzing past patterns and behaviors, organizations can make informed decisions, reduce uncertainties, and improve overall operational efficiency.
Lee et al. (2022), in their study on decision tree models, revealed that predictive analytics significantly improves forecasting accuracy and supports data-driven decision-making. Their research demonstrated that decision trees are particularly effective in identifying patterns, classifying data, and predicting potential outcomes in business environments. The study further emphasized that these models are widely adopted due to their simplicity, interpretability, and reliability in handling complex datasets.
Meanwhile, Gonzales et al. (2024) examined the application of machine learning algorithms such as Random Forest and Logistic Regression in predicting market demand. Their findings showed that these models greatly enhance forecasting accuracy by analyzing large volumes of data and identifying hidden trends. As a result, businesses are able to optimize inventory management, improve resource allocation, and respond more effectively to market fluctuations.

These studies collectively prove that predictive analytics can significantly enhance the FishLodger system by enabling accurate demand forecasting, improving supply planning, and supporting data-driven decision-making within the Lucena Fish Port Complex. By incorporating predictive capabilities, the system can assist stakeholders in anticipating market trends, minimizing losses, and maximizing operational efficiency.
Synthesis
The related literature and studies that were reviewed highlighted the growing importance of fish market monitoring systems, the effectiveness of progressive web applications (PWA), the role of supply chain traceability, the efficiency of automated reporting systems, and the increasing significance of predictive analytics in enhancing market operations and decision-making processes.
Fish market monitoring systems play a crucial role in improving transparency, efficiency, and accuracy in market operations by replacing traditional manual processes with digital solutions. These systems enable up-to-date identification of transactions, pricing, and inventory, which are essential in maintaining reliable market data. However, challenges such as limited technological infrastructure, resistance to digital adoption among stakeholders, inconsistent data recording practices, and lack of centralized systems continue to hinder the full implementation of digital monitoring in fish ports. These issues are further compounded by operational inefficiencies, including delayed reporting, data redundancy, and difficulty in accessing historical records. To address these 

challenges, the integration of a centralized and user-friendly monitoring system is necessary to streamline operations and ensure data accuracy within fish market environments.
The reviewed literature also supports the use of Progressive Web Applications (PWA) as an effective platform for delivering accessible and scalable information systems. PWAs provide advantages such as offline functionality, cross-platform compatibility, and improved system performance, making them suitable for environments with unstable internet connectivity. Despite these benefits, some limitations remain, including low awareness among users, initial adaptation challenges, and the need for proper system design to ensure usability. Nevertheless, PWAs offer a practical solution for implementing digital systems in fish port operations, as they allow stakeholders to access information anytime and anywhere while maintaining system efficiency and reliability.
Supply chain identification and traceability systems were also identified as essential components in modern fish market operations. These systems enhance transparency by allowing stakeholders to track the origin and movement of fish products from source to market. They also improve accountability, food safety, and consumer trust. However, challenges such as fragmented data systems, lack of integration among stakeholders, and limited technological resources affect the effectiveness of traceability systems. Addressing these issues requires the development of an integrated platform that combines 

monitoring and identification functionalities to provide comprehensive and accurate supply chain information.
Predictive analytics was identified as a key driver in optimizing market strategies and improving decision-making. Studies have shown that predictive models using machine learning algorithms such as Decision Tree, Random Forest,  and Logistic Regression can effectively forecast demand, identify trends, and optimize resource allocation. These tools enable proactive planning and help organizations respond to potential challenges before they occur. This highlights the need for systems that incorporate predictive capabilities to maximize the value of collected data.
The foreign and local studies gathered by the researchers have been instrumental in building the foundation for the proposed study entitled “FishLodger: A Progressive Web Application Fish Market Monitoring System with Retailer Information, Supply Source Identification , Automated Reports, and Predictive Analytics for Lucena Fish Port Complex.” The proponents utilized the reviewed literature to conceptualize a solution that addresses the existing gaps in fish market operations, particularly the lack of integration among monitoring, identification , reporting, and analytics systems. These insights significantly influenced the design and development of a comprehensive platform that aims to improve efficiency, enhance transparency, and support data-driven decision-making within the Lucena Fish Port Complex.
